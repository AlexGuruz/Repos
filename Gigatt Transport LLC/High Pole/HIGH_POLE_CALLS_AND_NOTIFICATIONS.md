# High Pole — CB Radio Calls & Required Notifications

**Route:** Tennessee → Arkansas (I-40, I-430, I-30, AR-108, US-71, AR-32, AR-41) → Texas (SH0008 AR Line → … → IH20NFR)  
**Load:** Over-height (high pole escort required). Use caution at all railroad crossings.

This sheet lists **(1) what to say on the CB and where**, and **(2) who to call (phone) and when** for this route. Regulations and contacts change; confirm with your permit holder and state DOT before the run.

---

## 1. CB RADIO — Channel & What to Say

- **Channel:** **19** (standard highway/truck channel). Confirm with your pilot/escort before rolling.
- **Keep it short.** Pilot cars want clear, minimal traffic: lane status, stop, cleared.

### Phrases to use (when and where)

| When / Where | What to say (examples) |
|--------------|------------------------|
| **Before entering a highway or merging** | *“High pole coming on I-30 west at mile ___”* or *“Merging on the right”* — so truck knows lane and traffic. |
| **Lane clear for the load** | *“Left lane clear”* / *“Right lane clear”* or *“I have the left lane for you”* |
| **Before any turn or exit** | *“Coming up on the turn”* → then *“Stop”* when needed → *“Cleared the turn”* when load is through. |
| **Traffic behind** | *“18 wheeler coming up”* (or “four-wheeler”) so driver knows to hold or move. |
| **Stop / go** | *“Stop”* when load must hold; *“Clear”* or *“Cleared”* when it’s safe to go. |
| **At railroad crossings** | *“Railroad crossing ahead — high pole”* so everyone is alert; after crossing: *“Cleared the crossing.”* |
| **At detours / tight spots** | *“Detour coming up”* then lane/stop/clear as above. |

### Where on this route to use the CB (by segment)

- **Arkansas:** I-40 WB → I-430 SB (Maumelle, Exit 147) → I-30 WB (Little Rock) → AR-108 WB (Texarkana, Exit 7) → US-71 NWB (Ogden) → AR-32 WB (Ashdown) → AR-41 SB (Wallace) to state line.  
  Call lane clear, stop, cleared at every turn/merge and **at every railroad crossing** and **before the three restricted bridges** (see below).

- **Texas (from SH0008 AR Line):**  
  - **Merges:** US67 sw (~mi 19), IH30 w (~mi 52), IH20 w (~mi 152), SH183 w (~mi 211), SH121 w (~mi 215), IH820 w (~mi 221).  
  - **Turns:** Every turn in the turn-by-turn (e.g. onto FM1840, SH98, BU271E, IH30NFR, SH24, US67 BUS/SH34, Murphy Dr, SH121, FM1938, IH820, US377, SH171, IH20NFR).  
  - **Detours:** Exit 141 (FM-900/Saltillo Rd), Exit 127 (TX-301/US-67 BUS), Exit 97 (Lamar St), Exit 93 (SH34/Wesley St), Exit 18 (Haltom Rd/Beach St).  
  - **Exits:** Exit 101 (SH24/FM1737), Exit 453A (TX-360 N), SH183, FM157/Industrial Blvd, SH26/FM1938/Davis Blvd/Colleyville, Exit 429A (US377/Granbury).

Use the CB at each of these so the driver gets “lane clear,” “stop,” and “cleared” at every merge, turn, and detour.

## 2. PHONE / NOTIFICATION CALLS — Who to Call & Where

### A. Railroad crossings (use caution; call in emergency or when required)

The **Texas permit** says: *“Use caution at all railroad crossings. In case of emergency, contact the appropriate railroad.”*  

Many jurisdictions also require **advance notification** (e.g. 24–30 minutes) before crossing with an oversize load. Check the **blue-and-white ENS (Emergency Notification System) sign** at each crossing for the **DOT crossing ID** and the **railroad’s phone number** — that’s the number to call for that crossing.

**Railroad emergency / coordination numbers (from permit and common use):**

| Railroad | Phone | When to use |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Amtrak | 800-331-0008 | Emergency / if required for crossing |
| BNSF Railway | 800-832-5452 | Emergency / coordination (e.g. 24 hr ahead where required) |
| CSX | 800-232-0144 | Emergency / coordination |
| Canadian National | 800-465-9239 | Emergency / coordination |
| Canadian Pacific | 800-716-9132 | Emergency / coordination |
| Kansas City Southern | 800-892-6295 | Emergency / coordination |
| Norfolk Southern | 800-453-2530 | Emergency / coordination |
| Union Pacific | 888-877-7267 | Emergency / coordination (24/7) |

**Where on your route:**  
- **Arkansas:** I-40, I-430, I-30, AR-108, US-71, AR-32, AR-41 all have or can have railroad crossings. Call at each crossing per ENS sign or permit instructions.  
- **Texas:** I-30, I-20, and other segments on your turn-by-turn may cross railroads; same rule — use ENS sign or the list above for the owning railroad.

If your permit or a jurisdiction requires “call before crossing,” do it at the **specific crossing** (e.g. “I-30 at mile X” or “AR-41 at MP Little River 10.89”) using the number for that crossing or the railroad above.

### B. Arkansas — Restricted bridges (CB + speed / spacing)

Your **Arkansas permit** requires **no other trucks within 1,000 ft** and **reduce speed to 5 MPH** at these bridges:

| Location | Where you’ll be calling / using CB |
|----------|------------------------------------|
| **Bridge 05320** | I-430 in Pulaski Co at 9.868(21) — River Mt Rd, RR, Ark River |
| **Bridge 05141** | I-40 in Crittenden Co at 283.859(52) — Miss River, CR, CI St, RR |
| **Bridge 03408** | SH 41 in Little River Co at 9.27(03) — Red River Relief |

**What to do:**  
- **CB:** Before each bridge: *“Restricted bridge ahead — 5 mph, no trucks within 1000 feet.”* After clearing: *“Cleared the bridge.”*  
- **Phone:** No routine “call” required for these bridges on the permit; use railroad/emergency numbers only if there’s an incident or if your permit holder says otherwise.

### C. Utility (power company) notifications — before the trip

For **over-height loads**, many states and utilities require **advance written notification** (often **10+ days**) to electric utilities along the route. You must **carry proof** of notification; failure can mean fines.

**Who to contact (examples along your route):**

- **Arkansas:**  
  - **Entergy Arkansas** — covers much of AR (Little Rock, I-40, I-30 corridor). Find current contact: [entergy.com](https://www.entergy.com) or state DOT.  
  - **SWEPCO (Southwestern Electric Power Co)** — 1-888-216-3523 (customer service); 1-888-218-3919 (outages). Ask for “oversize load notification” or “right-of-way / line clearance.”  

- **Texas:**  
  - **Oncor** — 888-313-6862 (Mon–Fri 8a–6p CT); contactcenter@oncor.com. Common along I-20, DFW, North Texas.  
  - **AEP Texas, TNMP, and others** — Vary by county. Use **TxDOT’s statewide utility contact list** (TxDOT Right of Way / UPS list) or **Texas PUC** to find the utility for each county you’re in.

**Where you’ll be (for utility notification):**  
- Arkansas: Crittenden, Pulaski, Saline, Hot Spring, Clark, Nevada, Hempstead, Little River (and others along I-40, I-430, I-30, AR-108, US-71, AR-32, AR-41).  
- Texas: From state line (e.g. SH0008 AR Line) through Texarkana area, Mount Pleasant, Dallas–Fort Worth (I-20, I-30, SH360, SH183, SH121, IH820), and to your Texas destination. Notify every utility whose lines are along the route; they’ll tell you if you need a letter or escort.

### D. Jurisdictions that require prior notification (Texas)

Texas has a **“Known Jurisdictions with Restrictions Requiring Notification Prior to Movement”** list (TxDMV). It includes cities/counties that require **advance phone or written notice** before you enter.  

- **Where:** Check [TxDMV – Permit contacts for city, county and TxDOT offices](https://www.txdmv.gov/motor-carriers/oversize-overweight-permits/permit-contacts-for-city-county-and-txdot-offices) (or latest TxDMV permit page) for the current list.  
- **When:** Per each jurisdiction (e.g. 24 hours, 30 minutes). Call **before entering** that city/county. Your route touches Texarkana, Mount Pleasant, Greenville, Dallas/Fort Worth area, and others — verify each one on the list.

## 4. Arkansas permit reminders (from your permit)

- **Little Rock curfew:** No oversize movement 7–9 AM and 4–6 PM on I-30 (MM 138–143), I-40 (MM 151–155), and all of I-630.  
- **I-40 movement hours:** No movement between exit 135 and 216 from 8 PM to 6 AM.  
- **Weigh stations:** Stop at open weigh stations and report with the permit.  
- **Escorts:** Front pole car with clearance bar 6" above load height; two escorts (front + rear) for length; rear escort for width on interstates.  

Use the CB to coordinate with escorts at curfew boundaries and before restricted bridges and crossings.
