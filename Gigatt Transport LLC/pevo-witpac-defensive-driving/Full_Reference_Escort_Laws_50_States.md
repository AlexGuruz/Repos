# PEVO / Pilot Car / Chase Car / Escort Vehicle Laws – All 50 United States

## Pilot | Chase | High Pole | WITPAC | General Operations

*Supplementary reference—equipment, escort dimensions, high pole by state. For PEVO/WITPAC/Defensive Driving credentials, see [README](README.md).*

> **Last compiled:** February 2026  
> **Disclaimer:** Laws and regulations change frequently. Always verify current requirements with each state's DOT or permit agency before operating. This document aggregates publicly available information and may not reflect the most recent rule changes.

---

## Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Pilot** | Lead or front escort vehicle—travels ahead of the load to warn oncoming traffic |
| **Chase** | Rear escort vehicle—travels behind the load to warn overtaking traffic (also called "rear escort," "trail escort," or "flag car") |
| **High Pole / Height Pole / Hi-Pole** | Measuring device mounted on the front escort to detect overhead obstructions; extends above load height |
| **WITPAC** | Wind Industry Transportation Professional Advanced Certification—specialized certification for wind turbine component hauling |
| **PEVO / P/EVO** | Pilot/Escort Vehicle Operator—certified escort driver |

---

## Federal Overview

- There are **very few federal rules** governing pilot/escort operations.
- Regulations are **mostly state-level**, with significant variation between states.
- FHWA and AASHTO have best-practice guidelines but no nationwide mandatory standards.
- Each state issues permits that specify route, dates, times, number of escorts, and other conditions.
- Source: [FHWA – Pilot Escort Operator and Vehicle Equipment Requirements](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwahop16050/m2.htm)

## When Escort Vehicles Are Generally Required

| Dimension | Typical threshold | Typical requirement |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Width** | >12–13 ft | 1 escort (front or rear depending on road) |
| **Width** | >14–16 ft | 2 escorts (front and rear) |
| **Height** | >13 ft 6 in (East) / >14 ft (West) | 1 escort with height pole |
| **Height** | >14–16 ft | 2 escorts; front with height pole |
| **Length** | >90–100 ft | 1 escort |
| **Length** | >100–125 ft | 2 escorts (varies by state) |

**Important:** Exact dimensions differ by state. The permit for each move states the escort requirements.

## WITPAC Certification

**WITPAC** (Wind Industry Transportation Professional Advanced Certification) is a specialized industry certification for pilot/escort operators, transport drivers, steerpersons, and related personnel moving **wind turbine components**.

### WITPAC Prerequisites

To earn full WITPAC certification, you must have **one** of the following:

- **Valid P/EVO certification** from: Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Minnesota, North Carolina, Oklahoma, Utah, Virginia, or Washington  
  **OR**
- **Class A CDL** from any U.S. state, Canadian province/territory, or Mexican LFC

### WITPAC Course Structure

- Self-directed online prep course (4–6 hours)
- 8-hour instructor-led session (teamwork, communication, route surveys, P/EVO duties, steerperson responsibilities)
- Written knowledge exam (typically 90% pass required)
- Valid for **3 years**; recertification available

### WITPAC vs State PEVO

- **WITPAC is not a substitute** for state PEVO certification where required.
- WITPAC is an **additional** credential focused on wind industry transport.
- States do not independently "accept" WITPAC—you still need a valid PEVO or CDL per state rules.
- Providers: Evergreen Safety Council, HASC (Colorado), and other approved trainers.

## High Pole (Height Pole) Requirements by State

The **height pole** (hi-pole) is mounted on the **front/lead escort** to detect overhead obstructions. Requirements vary by state.

| State | Height pole required when load exceeds | Pole clearance above load | Notes |
|-------|--------------------------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| **Alabama** | 16 ft | — | 1 escort for loads >16 ft high; pole typically on lead |
| **Colorado** | 16 ft | 6 in | Route survey required >17'6"; licensed signal contractor >17' through intersections |
| **Florida** | 14 ft 6 in | 6 in | Lead escort precedes load; law enforcement + qualified escort >16 ft |
| **Illinois** | 14 ft 6 in | — | Mounted on lead escort; 3 civilian + State Police >18 ft |
| **Louisiana** | 15 ft 10 in | — | 1 front escort with height pole; verify PEVO cert requirement |
| **Massachusetts** | 13 ft 8 in | — | >13'8": survey + 1 front escort; >13'11": 2 escorts; >14'11": 2 front + police |
| **Minnesota** | Permit-dependent | — | Check permit; no explicit height pole rule in general provisions |
| **Missouri** | 15 ft 6 in | — | **Divided highways only.** Route survey + 1 escort; utility bucket truck may be required |
| **New York** | 14 ft | 3 in (≤12 ft wide) / 6 in (>12 ft wide) | Lead escort; 3,000 ft distance for stopping |
| **Ohio** | 14 ft 6 in | — | 1 escort >14'6"; 2 escorts (lead + rear) >14'10" |
| **Texas** | 17 ft | — | Front escort with height pole; nonconductive; >18 ft requires 2 escorts |
| **Utah** | **16 ft** | — | R909-2-24; nonconductive, flexible material required |
| **Virginia** | 14 ft 5 in | 3 in | Mounted on front of lead escort |
| **Washington** | 14 ft 6 in | 3–6 in | Lead pilot/escort; pole must be nonconductive, flexible tip |

**General:** Pole must be nonconductive, flexible/non-destructive, and securely mounted. Do not display when not escorting. Many states fine for improper use. Thresholds may vary by route; permit conditions take precedence.

## Pilot (Front) vs Chase (Rear) Escort Requirements

### When Front/Pilot Escort Is Required

Typically required for:

- Overheight loads (front escort carries height pole)
- Overwidth loads on two-lane highways
- Front overhang >15–20 ft (state varies)
- Some overlength moves on two-lane roads

### When Rear/Chase Escort Is Required

Typically required for:

- Overwidth loads on divided/multilane highways
- Overlength loads (often rear on multilane)
- Rear overhang >15–20 ft
- When driver cannot see 200 ft to rear (some states)

### Example State Rules

| State | Front escort | Rear escort |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| **Colorado** | >85 ft (mountainous 2-lane); >110 ft (non-mountainous 2-lane); front overhang >15 ft | >115 ft on 4-lane |
| **Florida** | Width >12 ft on 2-lane; overheight (with pole); length >95 ft in some cases. *Exception:* 3-point articulated up to 105 ft = no escort | Width >12 ft on 4-lane; length >95 ft; 2 escorts when >150 ft |
| **Minnesota** | On undivided: >110 ft length OR >15–16 ft width | On divided: >110 ft length OR >15–16 ft width; 2 escorts >150 ft |
| **Ohio** | Height >14'6" (with pole); width >13 ft on 2-lane | Height >14'10" (2 escorts); width >14'6" |
| **Washington** | >11 ft wide on 2-lane (2 escorts); >14'6" height (with pole); front overhang >20 ft | >11 ft wide; >105 ft trailer; >125 ft on multilane |
| **Virginia** | >14'5" height; front overhang >10 ft; >12 ft width on noninterstate; >14 ft width | Rear overhang >15 ft; >85 ft noninterstate; >120 ft interstate |
| **Texas** | Width 14–16 ft on 2-lane; height >17 ft (with pole); length 110–125 ft on 2-lane | Width 14–16 ft on divided; length 110–125 ft on divided; rear overhang >20 ft |

## General Operator Requirements

- **Age:** 18 or 21 depending on state.
- **Certification:** Typically a one-day course and written exam; recertification every 3–5 years.
- **Insurance:** Often required (general liability, auto liability; Errors & Omissions sometimes recommended).
- **Appearance:** May not wear or display anything that resembles law enforcement.

---

## Recommended Equipment (CVSA / SC&RA / FHWA)

- Mileage log
- Oversize load signs (black on yellow; size per state)
- CB radios (two-way communication)
- Vehicle flags (red/orange fluorescent; 12" or 18" square)
- Emergency traffic control flags
- Reflective triangles (typically 3+)
- Flares
- Amber warning light (visible 500 ft; 360°)
- Hardhats and safety vests (ANSI compliant)
- STOP/SLOW paddle (18" minimum; 24" recommended for highway speeds)
- Fire extinguisher (5 B:C or equivalent)
- First aid kit
- Height pole (for loads >14–14.5 ft)
- Full-size spare tire and tools
- Flashlight, cones, mirrors

**State rules:** Some states require specific sign sizes, light bars, and minimum wheelbase.

## Example State Regulations

### Texas

- **Width 14'–16':** 1 front (two-lane) or 1 rear (divided)
- **Width >16':** 1 front and 1 rear
- **Height >17':** 1 front with height pole (nonconductive)
- **Height >18':** 2 escorts; front with height pole
- **Length 110'–125':** 1 front or 1 rear depending on road
- **Length >125':** 2 escorts
- **Vehicle:** 1,000–10,000 lb GVW; two flashing amber or one rotating beacon (8" min); blue+amber allowed (2019+)
- **Sign:** "OVERSIZE" or "WIDE LOAD"; 5–7 ft × 12–18 in; 8–10 in letters; yellow/black
- **Radio:** Two-way communication required
- Source: [TxDMV Escort Requirements](https://www.txdmv.gov/oversize-weight-permits/escort-and-equipment-requirements)

### New York

- **No escort:** <12 ft wide, <80 ft long, <14 ft high (unless slow-moving, overhang >10 ft, or bridge restrictions)
- **Front escort:** Two-lane when >12 ft wide, >80 ft long, or >14 ft high; height pole 3" or 6" above load
- **Rear escort:** Multi-lane when >12 ft wide, >80 ft long, or other conditions
- **Two escorts:** When both front and rear criteria apply
- **Three escorts:** >16 ft wide (two-lane), >18 ft (four-lane), or >100 ft long (two-lane), >160 ft (four-lane)
- **Police escort:** >200 ft length, >16–18 ft width, >200,000 lb, or >16 ft height
- **Certification:** Mandatory; **NY certification only** – no reciprocity
- **Equipment:** 100" wheelbase min, light bar (43–52" × 10–13" × 4–8"), signs, radio, triangles, flares, vest, stop paddle, flags, fire extinguisher
- Source: [NY DOT Escort PDF](https://www.dot.ny.gov/nypermits/repository/escort.pdf)

### Colorado

- **Height >16':** Front escort with height pole (6" above load); route survey >17'6"; licensed signal contractor >17' at intersections
- **Length:** Front escort >85 ft (mountainous 2-lane), >110 ft (non-mountainous 2-lane); rear escort >115 ft (4-lane)
- **Overhang:** Front >15 ft = front escort; rear >25 ft = rear escort
- **Vehicle:** Passenger or 2-axle truck; max 16,000 lb GVW; 60" min width
- **Equipment:** Height pole (nonconductive, flexible), signs on roof, amber lights (360°, 500 ft), flags, STOP/SLOW paddle, cones, flares, triangles, fire extinguisher, first aid
- Source: [CDOT Rules](https://www.codot.gov/business/permits/truckpermits/documents/rulesregsread.pdf)

### Washington (WAC 468-38-100)

- **Width >11 ft (2-lane):** 2 escorts (front + rear)
- **Width >14 ft (multilane):** 1 rear escort
- **Width >20 ft (multilane undivided):** 2 escorts
- **Height >14 ft 6 in:** 1 front escort with height pole (3–6" above load)
- **Length >105 ft trailer:** 1 rear escort (2-lane)
- **Length >125 ft:** 1 rear escort (multilane)
- **Front overhang >20 ft:** 1 front escort
- **Certification:** 8 hr initial / 4 hr renewal; 80% exam; 3-year renewal
- **Insurance:** $100K/$300K/$50K (BI/PD)
- Source: [WAC 468-38-100](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=468-38-100)

### Florida (FAC 14-26.012)

- **Width >12 ft:** 1 escort; >14 ft: 2 escorts (front + rear); >16 ft: law enforcement + qualified escort
- **Height >14 ft 6 in:** Lead escort with height pole (6" above load); >16 ft: law enforcement + qualified escort with pole
- **Length >95 ft:** 1 escort; >150 ft: 2 escorts (1 on limited access); >250 ft: law enforcement + 1 qualified
- **Length exception:** Truck tractor semi-trailers with **three points of articulation** up to **105 ft** allowed **without escort** (FAC 14-26.012(9)(b))
- **Qualified escort:** 8 hr defensive driving + 8 hr pilot/escort flagging; requalify every 4 years
- **Vehicle:** 2,000–26,000 lb GVWR; Class 2 amber lights; "OVERSIZE LOAD" sign; 2 flags (18" × 18")
- Source: [FAC 14-26.012](https://flrules.elaws.us/fac/14-26.012)

### Virginia

- **Height >14 ft 5 in:** Front escort with height pole (3" above load)
- **Width:** 1 rear >12 ft (interstate); 1 front + 1 rear >12 ft (noninterstate); 2 front + 2 rear >16 ft (noninterstate)
- **Length:** 1 rear >85 ft (noninterstate), >120 ft (interstate); 1 front + 1 rear >150 ft
- **Overhang:** Front >10 ft = front escort; rear >15 ft = rear escort
- **Insurance:** $750,000 minimum
- Source: [VA DMV Escort FAQs](https://dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/training/escrt/faqs-veh)

### Illinois

- **Height >14 ft 6 in:** 1 civilian escort with height pole on lead
- **Height >18 ft:** 3 civilian escorts + Illinois State Police
- **Length >110 ft:** 1 escort; >145 ft: 3 escorts
- **Width >14 ft 6 in:** 1 escort; >16 ft: 3 escorts
- **State Police:** Required for >18 ft height, >200 ft length, >18 ft width
- Source: [IDOT Oversize Permit Manual](http://idot.illinois.gov/Assets/uploads/files/Doing-Business/Manuals-Guides-&-Handbooks/Highways/Permits/Oversize%20and%20Overweight%20Permit%20Movements%20on%20State%20Highways%202015.pdf)

### Minnesota

- **Divided highway:** 1 rear escort >110 ft length OR >15–16 ft width; 2 escorts >150 ft length
- **Undivided highway:** 2 escorts (front + rear) >110 ft length OR >15–16 ft width; Peace Officer + 2 escorts when load extends past centerline
- **Certification:** MN or reciprocal (CO, FL, NC, OK, UT, VA, WA)
- Source: [MnDOT Escorts](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/cvo/oversize/escorts.html)

### Ohio

- **Height >14 ft 6 in:** 1 lead escort with height pole; **>14 ft 10 in:** 2 escorts (lead + rear)
- **Width:** 1 escort >13 ft; 2 escorts >14 ft 6 in; police escort >16 ft
- **Certification:** Not required
- Source: [Oversize.io Ohio](https://oversize.io/regulations/pilot-cars-escort-vehicles/ohio)

### Massachusetts

- **Height >13 ft 8 in:** Route/height survey + 1 front escort; **>13 ft 11 in:** 2 escorts; **>14 ft 11 in:** 2 front escorts + state police
- **Width:** 1 escort >12 ft; 2 escorts >13 ft 6 in
- **Certification:** Not required
- Source: [HeavyHaul.net](https://www.heavyhaul.net/massachusetts-permits/) / [Central Permits](https://centralpermits.com/state-regulations/massachusetts/)

## Notes

1. **Permits are the source of truth:** Each permit lists escort count, placement, height pole, and any special conditions. Permit conditions can override general thresholds.
2. **Reciprocity changes:** States update reciprocity agreements; FHWA table is 2017. Confirm with each state DOT before traveling. Some states require *residents* to hold in-state cert even when accepting out-of-state for non-residents.
3. **Equipment differences:** Sign size, flags, lights, and vehicle specs vary by state; meet the strictest applicable.
4. **Height pole:** Required on lead escort when load exceeds state threshold (~14–17 ft depending on state); pole typically 3–6" above load. Thresholds may vary by route type (highway vs. local).
5. **Communication:** Most states require two-way radio between escorts and load driver; CB is commonly used.
6. **Chase vs pilot:** "Chase" = rear escort; "Pilot" = front escort. Terminology varies by state.
7. **WITPAC:** Industry credential for wind hauling; requires valid PEVO or Class A CDL; not a substitute for state PEVO.
8. **Texas:** Flagger-style certification required; WA PEVO typically satisfies. TX Transp. Code §623.008 may impose additional traffic-direction training—verify.
9. **Wisconsin/Kansas:** Scope of requirements unclear; confirm with WisDOT and KDOT.
10. **Georgia:** Accepts Oregon certification (OR does not have its own full PEVO program).
