# Costs & Contacts — Validated February 2026

Course costs and provider contacts for PEVO, WITPAC, and defensive driving.

---

## Course Costs (Approximate)

| Credential | Cost | Validity | Notes |
|------------|------|----------|-------|
| **PEVO (Washington)** | $265+ | 3 years | 8 hr; online or in-person; 80% exam |
| **PEVO (Arizona)** | $225–245 | 4 years | Arizona Trucking Assoc.; member discount |
| **PEVO recert** | ~$90–200 | — | 4 hr typically |
| **WITPAC** | $325 | 3 years | 8 hr + online prep; $100 discount for renewal |
| **WITPAC private** | $2,200+ | — | On-site team training |
| **Defensive Driving (NSC)** | ~$30–80 | — | Online or classroom; FL accepts NSC DDC 10th ed |
| **Florida Pilot/Escort Flagging** | Varies | 4 years | 8 hr; FL vendor; requalify 4 hr |

*Verify current pricing with providers.*
