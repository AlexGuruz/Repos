# Defensive Driving – PEVO Requirements

Defensive driving requirements for pilot/escort operators.  
*Required by Florida and Pennsylvania; recommended for all PEVOs.*

> **Validated:** February 2026 | FL: 8 hr NSC or CDL | PA: defensive driving + flagger

---

## States Requiring Defensive Driving

| State | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Florida** | 8-hour NSC Defensive Driving (DDC 10th ed; online via SafetyServe OK) **or** valid Class A/B/C CDL. Required for qualified escorts; **mandatory for out-of-state PEVO** using reciprocal cert. Requalify every 4 years. [FL T2 FAQ](https://techtransfer.ce.ufl.edu/ufaqs/i-cant-find-the-8-hour-national-safety-council-online-defensive-driving-course-where-is-it/) |
| **Pennsylvania** | Defensive driving + flagger course required for PEVO on superloads. Accepts GA, CO/UT (RSA), NC, VA certs. |

---

## Course Standards

- **Florida:** Minimum 8 hours; instructor must be National Safety Council qualified (not employee of escort service)
- **Pennsylvania:** Defensive driving + flagger; specifics per PA DOT
- **Recommended:** 6–8 hour in-person (not online) for insurance discounts

---

## Recommended for All PEVOs

Even where not required:

- **Insurance:** Many carriers offer discounts for defensive driving completion
- **FHWA:** Recommends for PEVOs, especially over 55
- **Repeat:** Every 3 years suggested
