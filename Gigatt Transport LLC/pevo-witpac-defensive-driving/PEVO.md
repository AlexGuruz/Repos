# PEVO Certification – All 50 States

Pilot/Escort Vehicle Operator (P/EVO) certification requirements.  
*Training credentials only—no DOT permit or escort-dimension rules.*

> **Validated:** February 2026 | Source: ESC reciprocity (Sep 2025), FHWA, state DOTs

---

## States Requiring PEVO Certification

**14 states** require some form of PEVO certification.

*Reciprocity per ESC (Sep 2025). Confirm with each state before operating.*  
**Per-state checklist:** [State_Operations_Requirements.md](State_Operations_Requirements.md)

| State | Reciprocity / Notes |
|-------|---------------------|
| **Arizona** | Reciprocates with CO, FL, NC, UT, VA, WA. 4-year cert; Arizona Trucking Assoc. |
| **Colorado** | Accepts AZ, FL, MN, OK, UT, WA |
| **Florida** | Accepts AZ, CO, GA, MN, NC, OK, PA, VA, WA, WI. **Defensive driving required** for out-of-state |
| **Georgia** | Reciprocates with NC, FL, OK, VA, WA; accepts AZ, CO, UT (they don't reciprocate) |
| **Kansas** | Accepts WA; **KDOT registration** required for all PEVOs |
| **Minnesota** | Accepts CO, FL, NC, OK, UT, WA (AZ/GA not listed per MnDOT) |
| **New York** | **NY certification only**—does NOT accept any out-of-state cert |
| **North Carolina** | Reciprocity: FL, GA, OK, WA. Accepts: CO, MN, NY, NC, UT, VA |
| **Oklahoma** | Accepts CO, FL, GA, MN, NC, UT, WA |
| **Pennsylvania** | Required mainly on **superloads**. Accepts GA, CO/UT (RSA), NC, VA. **Defensive driving + flagger** required |
| **Texas** | Accepts WA PEVO (satisfies flagger). Flagger-style cert required; verify §623.008 |
| **Utah** | Accepts AZ, CO, FL, MN, NC, OK, VA, WA. Pilot vehicle permit required |
| **Virginia** | Accepts FL, GA, MN, NC, OK, UT, WA |
| **Washington** | Accepts AZ, CO, GA, MN, NC, OK, UT, VA. 8 hr initial / 4 hr renewal; 3-year cert |

**Partial / verify:** Louisiana, Nevada; New Mexico (escort vehicle permit); Kansas, Texas (limited programs); Wisconsin (scope unclear).

## General Operator Requirements

- **Age:** 18 or 21 depending on state
- **Course:** Typically one-day course + written exam
- **Recertification:** Every 3–5 years depending on state
- **Insurance:** Often required (general liability, auto liability)
- **Appearance:** No badge/uniform resembling law enforcement

---

## Reciprocity Caveats

1. Some states require *residents* to hold in-state cert even when accepting out-of-state for non-residents.
2. FHWA reciprocity table is 2017—states may have changed.
3. Florida and Pennsylvania require **defensive driving** in addition to PEVO—see [Defensive_Driving.md](Defensive_Driving.md).
