# WITPAC Certification

**Wind Industry Transportation Professional Advanced Certification**  
Specialized credential for pilot/escort operators, transport drivers, and steerpersons moving **wind turbine components**.

> **Validated:** February 2026 | Cost: ~$325 (renewal ~$225) | Validity: 3 years

---

## Prerequisites

To earn full WITPAC certification, you must have **one** of:

- **Valid P/EVO certification** from: Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Minnesota, North Carolina, Oklahoma, Utah, Virginia, or Washington  
  **OR**
- **Class A CDL** from any U.S. state, Canadian province/territory, or Mexican LFC

*Without these, you receive Proof of Completion and can upgrade within 180 days of obtaining PEVO.*

---

## Course Structure

| Component | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Prep** | Self-directed online (4–6 hours) + prep exam |
| **In-person** | 8-hour instructor-led session |
| **Exam** | Written knowledge exam (typically 90% pass) |
| **Validity** | 3 years; recertification available |

---

## WITPAC vs State PEVO

- **WITPAC is not a substitute** for state PEVO certification where required.
- WITPAC is an **additional** credential for wind industry transport.
- States do not independently "accept" WITPAC—you still need valid PEVO or CDL per state rules.
