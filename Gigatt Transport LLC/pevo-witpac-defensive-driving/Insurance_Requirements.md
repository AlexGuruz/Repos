PEVO insurance by state and industry recommendations.

## State Minimums (Where Specified)

| State | Minimum | Authority |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **Virginia** | $750,000 liability | Va. Code §46.2-2902 (escort vehicle services) |
| **Washington** | $100K / $300K / $50K (BI/PD) | WAC 468-38-100; for-hire operators |
| **Other states** | Varies; often not codified | Check permit conditions |

---

## Industry Recommendations (ESC)

*Bridge strikes and injuries can exceed millions. General auto policies may not cover PEVO activities.*

Carry these three:

1. **Professional Errors & Omissions (E&O)** — Covers injury/property damage beyond general liability (e.g., deficient performance without vehicle contact)
2. **General liability**
3. **Commercial automotive** — Vehicle damage

**Important:** Use an agent who understands PEVO work. Standard policies may only cover your vehicle, not bridge or third-party damage.
