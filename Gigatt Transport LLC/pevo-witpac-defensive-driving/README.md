## Credential Overview

| Credential | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **PEVO** | State-required certification to operate as pilot/escort vehicle driver (14 states require) |
| **WITPAC** | Industry certification for wind turbine component hauling; requires PEVO or Class A CDL |
| **Defensive Driving** | Required by FL and PA for PEVO; recommended for insurance and safety |

---

## Quick Reference

- **States requiring PEVO:** AZ, CO, FL, GA, KS, MN, NY, NC, OK, PA (superloads), TX, UT, VA, WA
- **Washington PEVO** — Widest reciprocity; accepted in 12 of 14 certifying states
- **New York:** NY certification only—no reciprocity
- **Florida:** PEVO + 8 hr NSC defensive driving (or CDL)
- **Pennsylvania:** PEVO + defensive driving + flagger (superloads only)
- **WITPAC:** Prerequisite = PEVO (from certifying state) or Class A CDL
