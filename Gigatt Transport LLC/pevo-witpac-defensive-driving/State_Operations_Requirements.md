# State Operations Requirements — Validated February 2026

What you need to **operate as a pilot/escort** inside each certifying state.  
*Cross-referenced with ESC (Sep 2025), FHWA, state DOTs. Verify with state before operating.*

---

## State-by-State Checklist

### Arizona
- [ ] PEVO from AZ, CO, FL, NC, UT, VA, or WA
- [ ] 4-year recert
- [ ] [Arizona Trucking Association](https://arizonapilotcar.com/pilotcarcert.html)

### Colorado
- [ ] PEVO from AZ, FL, MN, OK, UT, or WA
- [ ] [CDOT](https://www.codot.gov/business/permits/truckpermits/pilot-car-certification-information.html)

### Florida
- [ ] PEVO from AZ, CO, GA, MN, NC, OK, PA, VA, WA, or WI
- [ ] **8 hr NSC Defensive Driving** OR valid Class A/B/C CDL
- [ ] 8 hr Pilot/Escort Flagging (FL vendor)
- [ ] Requalify every 4 years
- [ ] [FL Tech Transfer](https://techtransfer.ce.ufl.edu/t2ctt/pe_faqs.asp) | [FAC 14-26.012](https://flrules.elaws.us/fac/14-26.012)

### Georgia
- [ ] PEVO from NC, FL, OK, VA, WA (reciprocal) or AZ, CO, UT (one-way)
- [ ] [GA DOT](https://www.dot.ga.gov/ps/permits/oversizepermits)

### Kansas
- [ ] Washington PEVO (or equivalent)
- [ ] **KDOT website registration** — obtain Kansas permit
- [ ] [KDOT](https://www.ksdot.org) — verify registration process

### Minnesota
- [ ] PEVO from CO, FL, NC, OK, UT, or WA (AZ/GA not listed)
- [ ] [MnDOT](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/cvo/oversize)

### New York
- [ ] **NY certification only** — no out-of-state accepted
- [ ] [NY DMV Escort](https://dmv.ny.gov/more-info/escort-driver-certification)

### North Carolina
- [ ] PEVO from FL, GA, OK, WA (reciprocal) or CO, MN, NY, NC, UT, VA
- [ ] [NCDOT](https://connect.ncdot.gov/business/trucking/pages/overpermits.aspx)

### Oklahoma
- [ ] PEVO from CO, FL, GA, MN, NC, UT, or WA
- [ ] OK DPS / ODOT

### Pennsylvania
- [ ] PEVO (superloads only)
- [ ] **Defensive driving + flagger**
- [ ] Accepts GA, CO/UT (RSA), NC, VA
- [ ] [PA DOT](https://www.penndot.pa.gov)

### Texas
- [ ] **Flagger certification** — WA PEVO satisfies
- [ ] Verify TX Transp. Code §623.008 for traffic-direction training
- [ ] [TxDMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/oversize-weight-permits/escort-and-equipment-requirements)

### Utah
- [ ] PEVO from AZ, CO, FL, MN, NC, OK, VA, or WA
- [ ] **Pilot vehicle permit** from UDOT
- [ ] [UDOT](https://connect.udot.utah.gov/business/motor-carriers/size-weight-permitting/pilot-escort-requirements-training-and-certifications/)

### Virginia
- [ ] PEVO from FL, GA, MN, NC, OK, UT, or WA
- [ ] **$750,000 minimum liability insurance** (Va. Code §46.2-2902)
- [ ] [VA DMV Escort](https://dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/training/escrt/faqs-veh)

### Washington
- [ ] PEVO from AZ, CO, GA, MN, NC, OK, UT, or VA
- [ ] **Insurance:** $100K/$300K/$50K (BI/PD) — WAC 468-38-100
- [ ] [WSDOT Pilot Car](https://wsdot.wa.gov/travel/commercial-vehicles/commercial-vehicle-permits/pilot-escort-vehicle)

## States Without PEVO Requirement

All other states do not require PEVO certification. Escort requirements are permit-dependent. See [Full_Reference_Escort_Laws_50_States.md](Full_Reference_Escort_Laws_50_States.md) for escort-dimension rules.
