## B. What each label means

**B1.** `REG 1 DEPOSIT` **/** `REG 2 DEPOSIT` **(cash sheet)**

- [x] A. Register sales / drawer cash counted **into** the cash pool
- [ ] B. Money already deposited at the bank
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B2.** `REG 1 BANK` **/** `REG 2 BANK` **(almost always about −$300)**

- [x] A. Till float kept in the drawer (**not** a bank deposit)
- [ ] B. Cash that was actually taken to the bank
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B3.** `ATM LOAD` **(cash sheet, money leaving cash)**

- [x] A. Cash taken from the pool and loaded into the ATM
- [ ] B. A bank fee or ATM fee
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B4.** `FROM BANK` **(cash sheet) and** `WITHDRAW` **(bank sheet)**

- [x] A. Same real event: cash pulled **from bank → into cash pool** (two sides)
- [ ] B. Different events that just often look similar
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B5.** `TO BANK` **(cash sheet) and** `DEPOSIT` **(bank sheet)**

- [x] A. Same real event: cash bagged **from pool → into bank** (two sides; bank may post 0–3 days later)
- [ ] B. Different events
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B6.** `SQUARE` **(bank sheet)**

- [x] A. Card sales settling **into the bank**
- [ ] B. Cash sales
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B7.** `SWITCH` **(bank sheet, money coming in)**

- [x] A. ATM / cash-network money settling **into the bank**
- [ ] B. Same thing as Square
- [ ] C. A fee or adjustment
- [ ] D. Not sure / Other: _________________________________

**B8.** `CHANGE` **(cash sheet)**

- [x] A. Drawer / change-fund adjustment
- [ ] B. Ignore / usually $0 placeholders
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________

**B9.** `BANK RECONCILE` **(cash sheet)**

- [x] A. Plug to force cash count to match reality
- [ ] B. Real bank transaction
- [ ] C. Other: _________________________________
